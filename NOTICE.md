# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [sel00000/csi-pose](https://github.com/sel00000/csi-pose/tree/02b1001590beea8e7d6daac57a4d8b4a345eb50f), snapshot `02b1001590beea8e7d6daac57a4d8b4a345eb50f`. The source is associated with ESP32-S3 WiFi pose-estimation research; the named authors are Kyung-Bo Kim, Hyun-Seok Jang, So-Hyeon Kim and Gyu-Chae Jung.

WiPose adds a command-line facade, source/CPU parity checks and documentation, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

The original MIT notice naming Kyung-Bo Kim, Hyun-Seok Jang, So-Hyeon Kim and Gyu-Chae Jung is restored in [LICENSE](LICENSE). New additions use [LICENSE-branding](LICENSE-branding). The retained [WiSPPN-derived model notice](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) reports that its upstream has no license file. Attribution alone does not establish a redistribution grant for those portions, and no separate grant was established by this review. Teacher models and other third-party components retain their own terms. The recorded pose and fall results in the reference guide are source-reported, not new measurements.
